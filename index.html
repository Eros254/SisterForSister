Sister For Sister Kenya is a national non-profit working side by side with vulnerable communities to achieve gender equality, promote education, and build futures — one girl at a time.

Founded

Our Mission

Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all girls and women in Kenya’s most vulnerable communities.

🤝

Our Approach

We work side by side with communities, governments, NGOs, and corporates — building knowledge and skills that lay foundations felt for generations to come.

✨

Our Vision

A Kenya where every girl child has the power, opportunity, and resources to shape her destiny and accelerate her dreams — regardless of where she is born.

Msamaria Mwema Child Empowerment

Our adopted home for girl child empowerment. We continuously support 60 school-going children with educational materials, books, uniforms, COVID masks, sanitary towels, and food donations.

Annual Need: KShs 7,470,000 (~$66,000)

Covid-19 Support

Distributing masks, sanitary supplies, and hygiene kits to children in homes and remote communities — keeping the most vulnerable safe.

KShs 5,000 / child / year

Ongoing

Educational Support

Providing school items including books, uniforms, stationery and learning materials to children at children’s homes and vulnerable households.

KShs 10,000 / child / year
