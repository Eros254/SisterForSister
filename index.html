Our Mission

To advance access to quality education, menstrual dignity, protection, and life-changing opportunity for girls and women in underserved communities across Kenya.

🤝

Our Approach

We work hand in hand with families, schools, community organizations, public institutions, and partners to deliver support that is practical, trusted, and rooted in local realities.

✨

Our Vision

A Kenya where every girl can learn, thrive, lead, and pursue her future with dignity, confidence, and the support she deserves.

Our Story

A grassroots movement growing into lasting change

Sister For Sister Kenya was registered in October 2014 and began with a simple but urgent response: helping girls stay in school with access to sanitary towels and educational support. Over time, that response has grown into a broader community-centered organization supporting children, caregivers, adolescents, and young women through education, dignity, advocacy, practical assistance, and partnership-led development.

Why we exist

Too many girls still face barriers that should never define their future: poverty, interrupted schooling, lack of sanitary support, and limited access to safe opportunities. We exist to help remove those barriers.

How we work

We listen first, partner locally, and respond practically. Our work combines direct support with community mobilization, referrals, advocacy, and relationship-building so impact can continue beyond a single intervention.

Who we work with

We collaborate with communities, children’s homes, schools, county and national institutions, ministries, NGOs, faith-based actors, and private-sector partners to build stronger, more sustainable support systems.

What Guides Us

Our Focus Areas

Our work is shaped by the everyday realities girls and families face, and by the practical systems communities need in order to move forward.

📚

Education Access

Helping girls remain in school through learning materials, uniforms, fees support pathways, and a stronger environment for academic progress.

🌸

Menstrual Dignity

Ensuring that menstruation never becomes a reason for missed school, shame, isolation, or reduced confidence.

🏥

Health & Wellbeing

Strengthening access to health information, hygiene support, reproductive wellbeing, and safer pathways to care.

🤝

Community Support

Working with caregivers, children’s homes, schools, and local institutions so responses are trusted, coordinated, and sustainable.

Why It Matters

When a girl is supported, her future opens up

Our work is about more than giving items. It is about restoring confidence, protecting dignity, and making sure girls can keep learning without barriers that should never define their potential.

“Support at the right time can change whether a girl falls behind or keeps moving forward with confidence.”

A reflection that guides our work with girls, caregivers, and community partners.

Practical Support

Uniforms, books, sanitary towels, food support, and digital learning tools all remove barriers that affect daily life and long-term opportunity.

Trusted Relationships

We work through homes, schools, caregivers, and partners because lasting change grows through trusted local relationships.

Longer-Term Possibility

Every intervention aims to move from immediate relief toward stronger wellbeing, education continuity, and life choices.
